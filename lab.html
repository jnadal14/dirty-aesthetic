Motion Lab

Animation ideas built with Motion, using the real artwork. Each one is tagged with the screen it is meant for. Try them on your phone, in Safari and Chrome, and through the Instagram bio link, then pick the ones that should go live.

Reduced motion is on for this device, so every demo shows its finished state without moving. That is exactly what visitors with that setting will see.

04

Stacked card deck

Each homepage section pins in place and the next one slides up over it like a card, while the one underneath sinks back and darkens. It is ordinary scrolling, nothing hijacked. On a phone held sideways the cards stop pinning (the screen is too short) and simply scroll.
